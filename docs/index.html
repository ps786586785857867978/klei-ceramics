Questions

I have never touched clay. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most people in our beginner workshops have never sat at a wheel. Your teacher shows every step, and nobody's first bowl is perfect.

What should I wear?

Clothes that can get dirty. We have aprons, and clay washes out easily.

Can I cancel or move my booking?

Yes, up to seven days before your workshop. Send us a message and we will find a new date.

Do you do private groups and team building?

We do. Tell us your preferred date and group size, and we will make a plan together.

New dates every month.

One email a month with new workshops, before they sell out.
